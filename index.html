FAQ

Questions people ask

Can someone find my phone number from the QR code?

No. The QR holds only a random code. Calls go through a virtual number that expires after 30 minutes, so neither side sees the other's real number.

Does the person scanning need the app?

No. The phone camera opens a web page. They pick a reason, verify their own number by OTP, and send an alert or call you.

What if I don't see the notification?

If you don't open the push in 60 seconds, we send an SMS. If it's still unread after 3 minutes, we send a WhatsApp message or an automated voice call.

What stops people from sending fake alerts?

Every reporter verifies their number. There are limits on how many reports one person can send, and you can mark an alert as spam or block the reporter.

I sold my car. Can I move the tag?

Yes. You can move a tag to another vehicle you own, or switch off a lost tag from the app.

Where is my data stored?

All personal data stays in India, encrypted. You can download or delete your data at any time, as required by the DPDP Act, 2023.
